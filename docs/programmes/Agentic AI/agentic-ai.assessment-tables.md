## Project rubric (as stored)
- **Task choice and step plan** (`task-design`): A real weekly task, measured now; every step marked AI, person, or AI then a person’s check, with a reason based on stakes and checkability
- **Briefs** (`briefs`): Every AI step has a brief with the goal, the organisation’s facts, audience, limits, an example and a format, improved after testing
- **Testing and verification** (`testing`): At least 5 varied tests (normal, messy, off-topic, tricky, personal data); the log shows each check, what was wrong and what changed
- **Privacy and safety** (`safety`): Owner’s written permission; only sample or redacted data reaches AI; sending, paying and deleting need a person’s approval; risks named
- **Handover and honesty** (`handover`): The owner can run it from the note; AI use disclosed plainly; the learner explains every step and what a person must still check
