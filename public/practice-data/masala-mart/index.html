Masala Mart practice data

Masala Mart is a fictional spice brand that sells online across India. These files are made up for practice in the MySkills programme AI-Powered Data Analytics. No real person or business is in them.

  • orders.csv: 2,030 clean orders, October 2025 to September 2026. Columns: order_id, order_date, customer_id, city, sku, quantity, order_value (₹), payment_method (UPI, Card, COD), returned (1 or 0).
  • orders_raw.csv: the same orders as a messy export, for cleaning practice: dd/mm/yyyy dates, cities typed different ways, “₹” amounts, some blank cities and some rows sent twice.
  • customers.csv: 800 customers. Columns: customer_id, city, signed_up, channel. Some never ordered.
  • products.csv: the price list. Columns: sku, product_name, category, pack_grams, price. Two new SKUs are not on it yet.

Open them free

  • Google Sheets: in cell A1 of a blank sheet, type =IMPORTDATA("https://myskills.org.in/practice-data/masala-mart/orders.csv")
  • SQL in your browser at shell.duckdb.org: CREATE TABLE orders AS SELECT * FROM 'https://myskills.org.in/practice-data/masala-mart/orders.csv';
  • Python in Google Colab: pd.read_csv("https://myskills.org.in/practice-data/masala-mart/orders.csv")
